## What this means for you

- The reels are **for personal use**. Do not publish them publicly without re-checking the footage, music and news terms.
- Do not commit rendered MP4s, downloaded clips or music to a public repository.
- AI-written reactions must stay labeled as AI. Do not present them as real engagement.
